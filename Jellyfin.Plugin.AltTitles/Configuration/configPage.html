Alternative Titles

Movies and series can be found under their other titles: in other languages, romanized (Japanese, Korean, Chinese) and as listed on AniDB for anime. The plugin adds a search provider to Jellyfin's search, so this works in the web client and in the apps. The titles are collected by the scheduled task "Collect alternative titles".

Sources

Language codes, separated by commas, e.g. "en, de". The title in each language comes from TMDB through Jellyfin's own TMDB provider; no key needed.
Romaji title, official titles and synonyms of every season, from AniDB's daily title list (matched through the Anime-Lists project). Downloaded at most once a day.
Revised Romanization, e.g. 기생충 → Gisaengchung.
With a free key from themoviedb.org (API key or read access token), every alternative title and translation TMDB knows is added: romanized titles of Japanese, Chinese and Korean films and dramas, working titles, titles in all languages.
